# Lesson 18: Exercise

## Plant your own silent bug

1. Take any earlier lesson workflow and plant a bug of the successful-and-wrong family: not an error, a wrong result (a field path one level off, a comparison against the wrong case, a branch wired to the wrong output).
2. Swap workflows with a study partner if you have one, or put it aside for a day and debug your own cold.
3. Debug strictly by the checklist, timing yourself, and recording the step at which the bug became visible.
4. Add a shape check at the boundary that would have converted your silent bug into a loud error, and prove it fires by re-planting the bug.

You are done when the shape check catches the replanted bug and your notes name the checklist step that found the original.
