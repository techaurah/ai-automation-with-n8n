# Lesson 28: Solution notes

A rehearsed rollback on a learning workflow usually takes two to four minutes, most of it finding the export and remembering the publish step, which is precisely the knowledge an incident demands at speed. The two failure points worth naming in the notes: importing creates a *copy* unless you overwrite deliberately, so the steps must say which workflow gets replaced; and a rolled-back workflow may need its credentials re-selected if the import landed on an instance where references differ (Lesson 13's references, doing their job). If your timed rollback exceeded ten minutes, the fix is filing, not skill: exports named by workflow and date, in the repo, beside the checklist.

**What to notice.** Nothing here was automated, and that was deliberate. Change control is a social technology that scripts support; Lesson 34 runs this exact routine on the capstone, where the stakes are a business's leads.
