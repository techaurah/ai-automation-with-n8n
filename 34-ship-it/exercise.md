# Lesson 34: Exercise

## The thirty-day letter

Write, in your project repo, a short letter to yourself dated thirty days from now (`DAY-30.md`): the four numbers you baselined and the thresholds that would worry you; the two design decisions you are least sure of and the evidence that would settle each; the graduation you most want to grant (the chaser sending without approval, a hosted model on one step, a second intake door) and the proof you will demand first; and one automation from your own backlog, scored by Lesson 01's three questions. Then set a calendar reminder for that date.

You are done when the letter exists, the reminder is set, and each graduation names its evidence. Courses end; operators write letters like this for the rest of their careers.
