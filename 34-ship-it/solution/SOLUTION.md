# Lesson 34: Solution notes

There is no model answer, and grading a letter would miss its point, but strong letters share a shape worth naming: the worries are *specific and falsifiable* ("chaser drafts reviewed by the owner fall below half opened" beats "engagement drops"), the unsettled decisions are the real ones from the build (most builders name the simulated classifier's swap and the status-advance timing), and each graduation's evidence is a measurement the system already produces, not a feeling scheduled for later. If your letter's evidence demands data the system does not collect, that is the letter's first action item, and Lesson 31 told you where it goes.

**What to notice.** You have finished a course whose final exercise is a promise to your future self, kept in the same repository as the system it describes. That is not sentimentality; it is the operating discipline of every module, applied one last time, to you.
