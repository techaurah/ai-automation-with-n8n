# Lesson 32: Exercise

## Write the qualification rubric

Lesson 33 scores leads with a rubric; this exercise writes it, because a rubric invented by the builder mid-code is a bias with syntax.

1. Define the scoring inputs a BrightSmile enquiry actually carries: category, channel, message signals (names a day, mentions pain, asks a price), and contact completeness.
2. Assign points to reach the course bands (hot 65+, warm 40 to 64, nurture below 40, on a 100 scale): every point must trace to a business reason a clinic owner would accept.
3. Write three example enquiries per band and hand-score them against your rubric. Any example landing in the wrong band means the rubric, not the example, changes.
4. Save the rubric as `RUBRIC.md` in your capstone folder; Lesson 33 implements exactly this document.

You are done when nine examples score into their intended bands and the rubric's reasons are stated in business language.
