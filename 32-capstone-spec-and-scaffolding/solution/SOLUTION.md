# Lesson 32: Solution notes

A defensible shape (yours will differ; the defence is the grade): category carries the most weight (a booking request is intent in plain text), urgency signals next (pain and named days are time pressure, which is when leads convert or evaporate), contact completeness after that (a lead you cannot reach is a lower score however keen), and channel least (it predicts reply speed, not value). Complaints exit the rubric entirely: they route to a human at intake (Lesson 08's angry branch, system-wide), because scoring a complaint is a category error the spec forbids.

**What to notice.** The rubric is a business policy that happens to be implemented in a Code node. Writing it as a document first, signed off in business language, is what makes Lesson 33 a build instead of an argument.
