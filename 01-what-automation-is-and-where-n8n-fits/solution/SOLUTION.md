# Lesson 01: Solution notes

Worked answers for the four script scenarios, and the pattern to grade yours against.

The enquiry greeter is a **workflow**: fixed steps, no judgement, moderate volume. The support inbox is an **agent inside a workflow**: the per-message decision is genuinely open-ended, but the frame around it (receive, log, escalate, reply) should stay fixed. The price recalculation is **code**: fixed steps at 1.2 million runs a month, where a visual canvas buys nothing and costs plenty. The invoice chaser is the trap: it feels agentic because the emails should vary, but the steps never change, so it is a **workflow with one AI drafting step**, and the drafting step gets a template, a tone guide, and a human approval rule in later lessons.

**What to notice.** The test rarely says "agent" alone. In practice the answer is almost always a workflow, sometimes with a model inside it. That is the economic centre of this whole field, and of this course.
