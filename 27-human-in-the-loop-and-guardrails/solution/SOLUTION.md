# Lesson 27: Solution notes

The test that matters is the third: guardrails sit *above* approval policy in precedence, because a blocked item is not a judgement call awaiting a decider, it is a policy violation, and letting any approver (human or automatic) wave it through reintroduces the skipping path the lesson exists to close. The auto-approver itself is legitimate design, not a cheat: graduated autonomy earned by category and value, with the log proving the pattern held, is how approval systems scale without training approvers to click yes reflexively.

**What to notice.** Every control in this lesson is a placement decision: checks before deciders, deciders before side effects, expiry meaning no. The models will keep improving; the placements are the engineering that survives them.
