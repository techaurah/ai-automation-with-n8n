# Lesson 31: Solution notes

Reviews done honestly on real estates almost always find the same three things: one legacy workflow consuming a third of executions to move data nobody reads anymore (retire it, and enjoy the only scaling win that is also a deletion), one workflow whose failure rate crept up behind a global average (its API changed a field in a minor release; Lesson 18's shape check is its fix), and model spend concentrated in a step a rules pre-pass or a smaller model handles at a tenth of the cost. The retirement defence writes itself in three currencies: executions and tokens saved, one less credential and failure surface to own, and an owner's attention returned.

**What to notice.** Monitoring ended in a deletion and two design fixes, not a dashboard purchase. The numbers are not the product; the decisions are, which is why this lesson taught reading before tooling.
